Search & answer: the RAG moment

Everything comes together here. We search the handbook for the 3 chunks that best match the question, paste them into the prompt, and ask Llama to answer only from those chunks. That is RAG: find first, then answer.

🧸 Think of it like this

An open-book exam. Before answering, you flip to the right pages and put them on the desk. Then you write your answer from those pages, not from memory.

🧪 Try this (guess first!)

  1. In step ③, why do we send only the top 3 chunks and not the whole handbook?
    AnswerA real handbook can be hundreds of pages. Sending only the best 3 chunks is faster, cheaper, and keeps Llama focused on the right part.
  2. Where did "22 days" come from? Is it written anywhere in the PDF?
    AnswerNot as one number. Llama read two chunks: "18 days" and "2 extra days for every 3 full years". 6 years = two blocks of 3, so 18 + 2 + 2 = 22.
  3. What could go wrong if we removed the words "ONLY from the context"?
    AnswerLlama might guess from what it learned about other companies, like "Most offices have a smart-casual dress code". It sounds right, but it is not in our handbook.
