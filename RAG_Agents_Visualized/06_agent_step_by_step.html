    🧪 Try this (guess first!)

    1. How many times did the LLM get called in this run?
      Answer4 times: three times it picked a tool (search, search, calculator), and the fourth time it wrote the final answer.
    2. Why not let the LLM just do 2010 − 1889 in its head?
      AnswerLLMs predict words, they don't really calculate. Easy sums usually work, but harder ones can go wrong. A calculator is always right.
    3. What would happen if the question needed 7 rounds?
      AnswerOur loop stops after 5 rounds, so the agent would stop without a final answer. That limit keeps it from running forever.
