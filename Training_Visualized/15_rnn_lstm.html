④ The LSTM: a notebook with 3 tools

🧽 Erase old notes. ✍️ Write new notes. 📢 Say what's needed now.

📖 “The boy is tall. The girls ___ …”
📓 The notebook remembers: “one boy”. Press ▶ Play all
🚪 Gate = a dial from 0 (closed) to 1 (open) 🛤️ The notebook slides along, so notes don't fade

⑤ Inside the LSTM cell: 4 gates

One cell runs for every word. Each gate is a small dial.

📖 “The boy is tall. The girls ___ …”  ·  now reading: girls
👆 Press ▶ Play all, or tap a gate card
σ = a dial: 0 closed … 1 open tanh = a value: −1 … +1 × = turn down · + = add 🧠📖 every gate reads the last output + the new word

📱💬

📚✉️
Long texts, early words matter
📓 LSTM
🌍🖥️
Huge amounts of text + big GPUs
⚡ Transformer

⑦ Then and now

Today's chatbots read all words at once.

⚡ Transformer: fast on GPUs, nothing fades 🔋 RNN: still great on tiny devices and live streams

⑧ From RNN to today's huge LLMs

40 years: from reading word by word to reading everything at once.

🔁 one word at a time ⚡ all words at once 🤖 huge + talks to everyone
  1. 🔁1986
    RNNReads one word at a time and keeps a small memory.
  2. 📓1997
    LSTMAdds the notebook and gates, so it remembers longer.
  3. 🌍2014
    TranslationOne LSTM reads a sentence, a second one writes it in another language.
  4. 👀2015
    AttentionWhile writing, the model may look back at any word.
  5. ⚡2017
    TransformerNo more reading one by one: all words look at each other at once.
  6. 📚2018
    GPT & BERTLearn from huge piles of text first, then reuse (like Lesson 11).
  7. 🐘2020
    GPT-3So big it can write stories, code and answers from a few examples.
  8. 💬2022
    ChatGPTA chat window: suddenly everyone can talk to an AI.
  9. 🤖Today
    Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama…Huge Transformers that read text, pictures and code.

🤔 Quick quiz

  1. An RNN reads 40 words. How many different cells does it use?
    AnswerJust one. The same 🔁 cell is reused for every word.
  2. 🧽 Forget gate = 0. What happens to the old note?
    AnswerIt is erased completely. (1 = keep it all.)
  3. A long story where the first words matter. 🔁 RNN or 📓 LSTM?
    Answer📓 LSTM. Its notebook keeps the early notes; the RNN's memory fades.
