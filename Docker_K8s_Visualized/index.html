🐳 Docker & Kubernetes, Visualized
Day 4 · interactive · works offline · ≈ 4 hours with the lab

From "works on my machine" to a self-healing cluster

Thirteen small pages that explain Docker and Kubernetes one idea at a time, each building on the one before, using our own image-classifier app from Days 2–3. Every page has a small simulation to play with, real command output from a real run, and a 🗣️ "Explain it like this" box with three sentences you can say to the class.

🧭 The story, in one line

😩 works on my machine→📦 image→▶ container→ 🧩 compose→🖥️ one machine isn't enough→☸️ cluster→ Pod→Deployment→Service→ ConfigMap→probes & limits→autoscaling→🧪 lab

Each step answers the problem the previous step left open. That's the order to teach it in.
